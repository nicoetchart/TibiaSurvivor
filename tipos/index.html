Damage types in TibiaSurvivor

In the Tibia MVP, combat uses elemental damage types (physical, fire, ice, energy, earth, death, holy). Each creature lists weaknesses and resistances in the bestiary. Typical in-run values: weak ×1.25, resist ×0.70, immune ×0 (no damage).
