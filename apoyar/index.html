Support TibiaSurvivor

TibiaSurvivor is and will stay free: no ads, no purchases and no pay-to-win. If you enjoy it and want to help keep it online, you can make a voluntary donation. All the money goes to the servers and the domain; anything left over is saved for the following months.

Donating gives no advantage in the game: everyone plays on equal terms.
