Objetos equipables de TibiaSurvivor

Durante la partida puedes encontrar objetos equipables en cofres y al derrotar élite o jefe. Solo uno a la vez; dura toda la run.

  • Starlight VialRegenerate 1.5% of your max HP per second.
  • Skull+40% damage, but you cannot switch spells.
  • Sun Catcher-20% cooldown on all spells.
  • Moon MirrorA killing blow leaves you at 1 HP instead (60 s cooldown).
  • Bone FiddleYou heal from the damage you deal.
  • Glowing Mushroom+50% gold coins at the end of the run.
  • Frozen Starlight+30% experience.
  • Magical Torch+35% movement speed.
  • Blue SphereMelee attackers take damage when they hit you.
  • Cursed Coin+20% damage reduction.
