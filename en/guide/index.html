TibiaSurvivor guide

TibiaSurvivor is a pixel-art fantasy survivor in your browser: you play a Master Sorcerer, roam an endless dungeon and your spells fire on their own while creature hordes close in. Each run lasts about 15 minutes up to the final boss.

Play free now

How to play

  • On PC: WASD or arrows to move. 1–4, Q/E or HUD click to switch spells. Esc pauses.
  • On mobile (landscape): drag to move; tap a spell icon to activate it.
  • The active spell casts itself; you dodge and position to avoid being surrounded.
  • Collect XP orbs, pick level-up cards and bind fused spells into one HUD slot.

MVP content

  • 16 creatures in the bestiary (orcs, undead, demons…).
  • 24 Master Sorcerer spells and passives with Tibia-style icons.
  • Elite sentinel (Minotaur Guard) and Demon boss at the end of the run.
  • Damage types: weaknesses and resistances per creature.
  • Coins, permanent upgrades, run items and missions / achievements.
  • Installable as an app from the browser.

Tips

  1. Loop in wide circles so hordes line up for chain spells.
  2. Check bestiary weaknesses and swap to the right element.
  3. Step out of ground circles and lines: telegraphed enemy attacks.
  4. Drop healers and shielders first (sentinel, ghouls…).
  5. Spend coins on permanent upgrades between runs.

Frequently asked questions

Is TibiaSurvivor free?

Yes. It is a browser fan game: no mandatory download and no pay-to-win in a run. You can support the project with voluntary donations (servers and domain).

Do I need to download anything?

No. Play in your browser on PC, tablet or phone (landscape works best on small screens). You can add it to your home screen as an app.

Which character do I play?

In the MVP you are a Master Sorcerer: damage spells, auras and utility you pick on level-up, survivor-style.

How long is a run?

Each run lasts about 15 minutes: creature hordes, an elite sentinel and a final boss if you last long enough.

How many creatures are there?

The MVP bestiary lists 16 creatures (orcs, undead, demons…), each with different damage weaknesses.

What are damage types?

Physical, fire, ice, energy, earth, death and holy. Your spells and enemy attacks use these; creatures resist or are weak to some (see the damage chart).

Is there gacha or tickets in a run?

Not in the Tibia MVP: floor loot is coins, XP and upgrades; neutral menu has no gacha or tickets.

Is my progress saved?

Yes: coins, permanent upgrades, bestiary and missions are saved in the browser (and in the cloud with an account). A run in progress is not interrupted by updates.

Is this an official Tibia game?

No. It is a fan game inspired by dungeon hunting; it is not affiliated with CipSoft.
