TibiaSurvivor

TibiaSurvivor is a free browser survival fan game. It is not affiliated with Tibia, CipSoft, or any commercial MMORPG.

Content and licenses

Code is released under the MIT license. UI font: Crimson Pro (SIL Open Font License 1.1). Music and SFX are original to this project. Creature fallback art is drawn in code. Outfits, tiles, items, and spell graphics may use PNG atlases exported from the Tibia game client for fan use only; raw client archive files are not distributed with the game.

Privacy

If you create a local account, we store your player name and save (coins, missions, achievements and stats) in this browser's storage. Nothing is sent to external servers. As a guest, nothing is saved after you leave.
