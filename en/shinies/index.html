Shiny collection (legacy mode)

In the TibiaSurvivor MVP there are no in-run shiny creatures: you play Master Sorcerer versus the Tibia bestiary. The /shinies/ URL is kept for old legacy-mode links.

If your save still has legacy collection or gacha, it remains in the game menu. For current content see the bestiary and spells.
